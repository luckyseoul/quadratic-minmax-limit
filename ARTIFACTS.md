## Unambiguous terminology

For a Paley reference `C_p` of order `n=p^2+1`, let
`delta_p = Phi(C_p) - m_n >= 0`.

`delta_p <= 2` is the strong uniform-gap target. `delta_p = o(p^3)` is
asymptotic reference optimality. The former implies the latter; the reverse
has not been proved. Combined with the proved asymptotic reference value
and ratio-dense orders, the latter suffices for `L=1/2`. Neither is known
necessary for convergence without specifying its value. Historical `E1`
notation is overloaded: inspect the explicit formula before reusing it.

Legacy `main_L_from_e1` fields are route-local implication diagnostics,
not global status. The authoritative original-problem registry is
`src/original_mo_status.py`. A reviewed proof through another route may
settle the original question even while every parked Paley lemma is open.
