# Experiment 4 — F(16) branches vs one-sided excess

## Hypothesis (H4)

1. \(F(16)=28\): sub-neutral at \(8\to16\), does not worsen \(E_8\), and
   \(E_n(16)\le n^{-1/2}\) for \(n\ge8\).
2. \(F(16)=30\): worsens \(E_8\) by \(>0.002\), still covered by \(n^{-1/2}\),
   super-neutral.
3. Only the \(28\) branch is sub-neutral.
