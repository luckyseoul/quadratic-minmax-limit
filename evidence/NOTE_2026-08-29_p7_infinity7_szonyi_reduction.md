# Proposition 15.713: a direction-theorem cut at p7 infinity plus seven

For the positive-product `p=7` boundary consisting of infinity and seven
finite points, both four-direction types use the phase-zero floor

```text
F_0(1)=F_0(3)=F_0(5)=8,  F_0(7)=0.
```

Projecting away the residue and quotient labels leaves all 35 multisets of
four `b` values per labelled type. Of the 1,225 ordered projected pairs,
1,217 satisfy the finite pair-deficit budget 42. This is an exhaustive outer
ledger of projected `b`-profiles, not a count of residue/quotient-labelled
arithmetic states.

For the seven-point affine boundary `B`, a direction with `b_d=7` has seven
singleton fibres and is not determined by `B`. If `z` counts these
directions, the exact arithmetic ledger has histogram

```text
z:        0   1   2   3   4   5  6  7  8
profiles: 217 300 280 210 126 56 21  6  1.
```

Szőnyi's theorem requires a noncollinear seven-point subset of `AG(2,7)` to
determine at least five of the eight directions. Hence `z>=4` forces `B` to
be collinear. A collinear seven-set is a complete affine line and has, up to
the two type labels, profile

```text
{1:1,7:3}/{7:4}.
```

Exactly those two labelled profiles survive among the 210 rows with `z>=4`.
The other 208 are impossible, reducing the positive branch's projected
`b`-profile envelope from 1,217 to 1,009. The negative branch and the remaining positive rows
are unchanged, so infinity plus seven and every top-level gate remain open.
No solver is used.

Targeted GitHub-code, MathOverflow, literature, and OEIS searches found the
general Szőnyi theorem but no prior occurrence of this exact `p=7`
phase-labelled application, histogram, or 1,217-to-1,009 reduction. The OEIS
appearance of `1217,1009` in an unrelated prime-polynomial sequence is a
numerical coincidence. This is context, not evidence or a formal priority
claim.
